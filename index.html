Instagram ad data lab

Upload your Ads Manager export or type your numbers. Gapfinder reads every ad like a funnel and tells you exactly what to fix.

  1. Do people stop?Hook rate: 3-second views divided by impressions. Shows if your first 3 seconds work.
  2. Do they keep watching?Hold rate: ThruPlays divided by 3-second views. Shows if the middle of the video holds attention.
  3. Do they click?Link CTR: link clicks divided by impressions. Shows if the offer and call to action are strong.
  4. Do they buy?Conversion rate and cost per result. Shows if your price, page or WhatsApp replies close the sale.

Add your data

In Ads Manager open Reports, switch to the Ads level, add the columns below, then Export as CSV.

Columns to include in your export

Ad name, Amount spent, Impressions, Reach, Frequency, 3-second video plays, ThruPlays, Link clicks, Results, and Purchases conversion value (or Purchase ROAS). For WhatsApp ads, Results = messaging conversations started; type your real orders in Orders when adding by hand.

    What each number means

    CPM
    What you pay for 1,000 views of your ad. Set by the country, season and competition, not by you.
    CPC
    Cost per link click. Falls when your CTR goes up.
    Frequency
    How many times the average person saw the ad. Above 3.5 people get bored and costs rise.
    Cost per order
    Spend divided by orders. Must stay below your profit per order before ads, or you lose money.
    ROAS
    Revenue divided by ad spend. A ROAS of 3 means $3 back for every $1 spent. Compare it to your break-even ROAS from the calculator.
